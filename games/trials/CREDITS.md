# Quarry Trials content provenance

Bike, rider, quarry kit, course design, graphics and PCM sound cues are original project-generated content. Python/headless Blender produces the bike/rider GLBs; source specifications and scripts are in `scripts/trials/`. Quarry materials/signs are drawn locally at runtime. No Trials game assets, recordings or branding are included.

The six WAV files are generated by `scripts/trials/audio.py` from deterministic harmonics and seeded noise. There are no sampled recordings or external audio dependencies. This provenance statement does not assign a new license to the repository's original content.

Third-party runtime code: Three.js 0.180.0 and matching loaders/utilities (MIT); Planck.js 1.4.2 (MIT). Their local license files and provenance are documented in `lib/README.md`. Local font licenses remain with `lib/fonts`.
