Privacy / jakehutson.me

Privacy, without the mystery.

This website is a personal site operated by Jake Hutson. This page explains what information may be processed when you visit the site and how the site's browser diagnostics feature works.

Information this site collects

This website does not currently provide user accounts, advertising or behavioural tracking and does not intentionally collect personal information simply because you visit the site.

Like most websites, normal technical requests involved in loading the site may include information such as your IP address, browser information and the page being requested. Hosting and third-party infrastructure providers may process this information as part of delivering their services.

Browser & Device Information tool

The Browser & Device Information page uses standard browser APIs to display information that your browser makes available to websites, such as browser type, operating environment, display information and supported browser capabilities.

The diagnostics are processed locally within your browser. The page does not request access to your location, camera or microphone simply by opening it.

If you choose Copy report or Export JSON, the report is created on your device. Copying places the report on your clipboard and exporting creates a local file for you to save.

Cookies and local storage

This website does not currently use non-essential cookies for advertising, analytics or behavioural tracking.

Apex Drift stores your sound preference and personal circuit records in your browser's local storage. This includes your best drift scores, fastest finishing times and completed-run counts. These records are not uploaded to a server. Clearing this site's browser data removes them. If local storage is unavailable, the game keeps records only for the current visit.

If this changes in the future, this privacy information will be updated and, where required, an appropriate consent mechanism will be introduced before non-essential technologies are used.

Third-party services

The site's frontend libraries, icons and fonts are hosted with this website. Your browser does not need to contact a third-party library or font CDN to load these resources.

The site's hosting provider processes normal technical requests to deliver the pages and their resources. External websites linked from this site have their own privacy practices.

External links

This site contains links to other websites, including GitHub. Once you follow an external link, that website operates under its own privacy practices and policies.

Contact

If you have a question about privacy or how this website works, you can contact hello@jakehutson.me.

Changes to this notice

This privacy notice may be updated when the website changes or new functionality is introduced.
