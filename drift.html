KNOW YOUR CONTROLS

How to play.

Three laps to put a score on the board. Hit all three yellow checkpoint pairs in order, then cross the chequered line.

Wor↑

Give it some

W or ↑ to accelerate. Get your speed up before the next bend.

Sor↓

Slow it down

S or ↓ to brake. Keep holding to back it up.

AD/←→

Pick your line

A / D or the arrows to steer. Rear stepping out? Steer the other way to catch it.

SPACE

Kick the rear out

Tap SPACE as you turn in. Let go, catch the slide and keep it moving.

Left thumb. Your line.

↑ to go. ↓ to brake, then reverse. Tap SLIDE as you turn in to get sideways.
