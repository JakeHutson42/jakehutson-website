About

Engineering and design belong in the same conversation.

I like software that feels considered - clear structure underneath, useful behaviour on the surface, and details that make it pleasant to use repeatedly.

Most of my work sits somewhere between application development, interface design and solving awkward practical problems. I am interested in the whole product, not just the code that makes it run.

This site is a record of that work. Some projects are polished products, some are useful tools, and others are simply ideas worth exploring.
